Fully Worked Examples

Below are complete step-by-step numerical solutions using the Improved Euler (Heun) Method.

Example 1

Solving \( \frac{dy}{dx} = x + y \)

Given

\[ \frac{dy}{dx}=x+y \] \[ y(0)=1 \] \[ h=0.1 \]
Step 1

Compute Initial Slope

Evaluate the differential equation at the initial point.

\[ f(x_0,y_0) = 0+1 = 1 \]
Step 2

Predictor Formula

Use Euler’s Method to predict the next value.

\[ y_p = y_n + h f(x_n,y_n) \] \[ y_p = 1 + 0.1(1) \] \[ y_p = 1.1 \]
Step 3

Compute Corrected Slope

Evaluate the function at the predicted point.

\[ f(x_1,y_p) = 0.1+1.1 = 1.2 \]
Step 4

Apply Heun Corrector Formula

\[ y_1 = y_n + \frac{h}{2} \left[ f(x_n,y_n) + f(x_{n+1},y_p) \right] \] \[ y_1 = 1 + \frac{0.1}{2} (1+1.2) \] \[ y_1 = 1.11 \]

Final Answer

\[ \boxed{ y(0.1)=1.11 } \]
Example 2

Solving \( \frac{dy}{dx}=y-x^2+1 \)

Given

\[ \frac{dy}{dx}=y-x^2+1 \] \[ y(0)=0.5 \] \[ h=0.2 \]
Step 1

Compute Initial Slope

\[ f(0,0.5) = 0.5-(0)^2+1 \] \[ f(0,0.5)=1.5 \]
Step 2

Predictor Formula

\[ y_p = 0.5 + 0.2(1.5) \] \[ y_p = 0.8 \]
Step 3

Compute Corrected Slope

\[ f(0.2,0.8) = 0.8-(0.2)^2+1 \] \[ f(0.2,0.8) = 1.76 \]
Step 4

Apply Heun Corrector Formula

\[ y_1 = 0.5 + \frac{0.2}{2} (1.5+1.76) \] \[ y_1 = 0.826 \]

Final Answer

\[ \boxed{ y(0.2)=0.826 } \]
